About Sixt Holdings

Building the systems, teams, and knowledge foundations that enable better decisions.

Sixt Holdings was established to help organizations solve complex problems through the integration of engineering, data, artificial intelligence, and human performance. Our work is grounded in the belief that sustainable innovation requires more than technology. It requires trusted information, sound architecture, capable teams, disciplined leadership, and a clear understanding of how organizations transform knowledge into action.

The company serves organizations seeking practical guidance in software modernization, data strategy, artificial intelligence, engineering leadership, organizational learning, and technical workforce development. Sixt Holdings also advances original research and professional education related to the Theory of Cognitive Infrastructure.
